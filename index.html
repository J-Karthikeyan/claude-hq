                How the creatures work
                
      
    
                
    
                
      
                🎲 Which Pokémon does a session get?
                
      
                  
        
                • Every Claude session has a unique ID. We hash it and take hash mod 48 to pick one of 48 species — so a
          session always maps to the same Pokémon, forever. It never changes as the session grows.
                  • 
        
                • The 48 slots are mapped to real Pokémon (Pikachu, Charizard, Umbreon, …), and each shows its real type
          (Charizard = Fire, Umbreon = Dark) with a type-colored badge.
                  • 
      
                
      
                🥚 → 🦎 → 🐉 How do they evolve?
                
      
                  
        
                • Each creature has a real evolution line (e.g. Charmander → Charmeleon → Charizard, or Magikarp → Gyarados).
          As a session grows, its Pokémon actually evolves through that line — the sprite and name change, they don't
          just get a label.
                  • 
        
                • Growth is measured by how many prompts you've sent in that session, in 5 tiers:
          0–2, 3–11, 12–29, 30–69, 70+. Those tiers map evenly onto the evolution line, so a
          2-stage line evolves once and a 3-stage line evolves twice as you go.
                  • 
        
                • The card shows the current form and "→ next form" with a progress bar; crossing an evolution triggers a
          little celebration. The Pokédex shows the furthest form you've evolved each species to.
                  • 
      
                
      
                ✦ How does shiny work?
                
      
                  
        
                • Shiny is a stable per-species trait (about 1 in 4 species) — so every session of a shiny species is shiny, and the Pokédex always matches your live cards. Which species are shiny is seeded per install, so your shinies are your own — two people in the same Arena don't share the same set.
                  • 
        
                • Shiny creatures show a ✦ mark, a gold shimmer, and their actual shiny-colored sprite (e.g. black Charizard).
                  • 
      
                
      
                📕 The Pokédex
                
      
                  
        
                • It scans all your sessions (past and present) and shows which of the 48 species you've encountered.
          Caught = you've had at least one session of that species; the count is how many sessions, and it tracks the
          highest evolution stage and total output tokens you've reached for it.
                  • 
        
                • Uncaught species show a greyed "???" silhouette — go start a session that hashes to it to catch 'em all.
                  • 
      
                
      
                🎓 Trainer level & sprites
                
      
                  
        
                • Your Trainer level is your overall season XP (from prompts, tool calls, and artifacts across 30 days).
                  • 
        
                • Sprites are the classic animated Gen-5 pixel art, loaded from the public PokéAPI library. The only thing that
          leaves your machine is a Pokédex number — never your conversation data. Offline, they fall back to a
          generated monster sprite. Switch packs anytime in ⚙️ Settings.
                  • 
      
                
      
                ⚡ Energy, rest & snacks
                
      
                  
        
                • About 2h of non-stop work makes a creature Fatigued 💦; past 3h it may faint 💫, and by 4h it will.
                  • 
        
                • Pauses under 5 min still count as work; after 10 min away it rests and recovers 4× as fast as it tires.
                  • 
        
                • A fainted creature wakes once its load drops below 2h (at most ~55 min of rest) or with a Revive Tonic.
                  • 
        
                • Snacks: 🫐 Berry 1🪙 −20m · 🍙 Rice Ball 2🪙 −45m · 🍱 Bento 3🪙 −2h · 🧃 Revive Tonic 5🪙 wakes it.
                  • 
        
                • 5 Poke Coins per UTC day (05:30 IST), and the purse holds 30; gift coins or snacks from the Arena tab.
                  • 
        
                • Which session ate stays on this machine. Energy is purely cosmetic: it never touches XP or the Arena board.
          Turn it off in ⚙️ Settings.
